DOG PARK
Pick the winner. Call the margin. Measure up.
  1. Pick the winner and call the margin. Your pick locks the moment the game starts.
  2. Scoring, the Direct Hit way: call the exact margin for 100 points. Every point you are off inside ten costs 10. Miss the winner or land outside ten and it scores 0. No negatives, ever. On a UFC fight, call the winner and the round it ends.
  3. One account per person. Members verify with an email code, so the Park's numbers stay honest: no fake players, no spam, no stuffing the Yardstick. Your username becomes the name on your Score Card, and once it is verified it is yours alone, so nobody can be mistaken for you.
  4. One stamp is your registration. Pick one name, stamp it once, and it shows with an @ everywhere: your Score Card, the Park Board and your picks. Guests play free on their own card, and guest picks stay on your own card only and never touch the community numbers. Register your stamp with an email code and it is yours alone in every browser: your picks count in the Park Yardstick and standings, and talking in the Park unlocks.
  5. Keep usernames clean. The Park is for everybody.
