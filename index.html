PARK FAQs
The questions every new pup asks.
Is Dog Park free?
Yes. Free to play, forever. No entry fees, no cash prizes, bragging rights only.
How do I play?
Pick the winner of each game and call the winning margin with the slider. Your pick locks the moment the game starts. On a UFC fight, pick the winner and the round it ends.
How does scoring work?
The Direct Hit way: call the exact margin for 100 points. Land inside ten and every point you are off costs 10. Miss the winner or land outside ten and it scores 0. No negatives, ever. UFC: exact round 100, one round off 50, right winner with the round missed 25.
What is the Yardstick?
The Park's projected margin. It starts at the posted line and every member pick moves it. Once a game has three or more member picks, the Park Yardstick becomes the line you play against.
Why join the Park?
Guest picks stay on your own device. Members verify with an email code, and their picks count in the Yardstick and the standings. Your verified @name is yours alone, nobody else can take it.
Can I change my card name?
Guest names stamp once and lock, like a dog tag. When you verify, your @name takes over the tag for good.
What are season codes?
Your Score Card makes a DP code. Trade codes with friends and their cards join your Park Board, yours joins theirs.
Is this gambling?
No. No money in, no prizes of value out. Points, trophies and bragging rights only.
